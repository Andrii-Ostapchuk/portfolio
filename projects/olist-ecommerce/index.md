# Olist E-Commerce Data Warehouse & BI Pipeline

## Business Context:
Olist is a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace with 100k+ orders characterized by a low (~3%) repeat-purchase rate and complex multi-seller logistics.

## Objective:
Build an end-to-end data pipeline and BI reporting layer to monitor seller performance, logistics costs, and customer lifetime value.

## Architecture:
Bronze (Raw Ingestion) → Silver (Cleansing & Integrity) → Gold (Star Schema Dimensions/Facts) → Reporting (Pre-Aggregated Analytical Views)
